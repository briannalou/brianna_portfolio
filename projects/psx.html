MoMA PSX

2025 Branding Digital

PSX is a speculative rebrand of MoMA PS1 that explores how a visual identity can reflect the museum’s experimental and constantly evolving character. The project aims to move beyond a fixed institutional image and create a flexible system that can adapt across exhibitions, events, digital platforms, and physical environments.

Role Brand Designer
Tools Figma, Illustrator
Inspo

The identity draws inspiration from MoMA PS1’s industrial architecture, modular exhibition spaces, and its role as a platform for emerging and experimental art.

'X' represents transformation and the unknown, representing a space where different artists, audiences, and ideas intersect.

Concept

The system can extend, distort, separate, and reconnect.

Concept

PSX can maintain a recognizable identity while continuing to change across different programs, spaces, and audiences.
