Moma PSX

2025 Branding Digital

A brand identity and digital experience exploring the intersection of gaming culture and contemporary art, developed for a MoMA special exhibition.

Role Brand Designer
Tools Figma, Illustrator
Concept

MoMA PSX grew out of a tension between institutional gravity and the irreverent energy of early gaming culture. The brand identity had to hold both — serious enough for the museum context, strange enough to feel true to the subject matter.

Outcome

A visual system that borrowed from hardware packaging, pixel art conventions, and exhibition design — applied across digital environments, printed collateral, and wayfinding for the gallery space.
