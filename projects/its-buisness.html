Its Buisness

2025 Digital Print Illustration

A deadpan illustration series treating the absurdities of work culture with the same visual seriousness usually reserved for things that matter — because sometimes the joke is in the sincerity.

Role Illustrator
Tools Procreate, Illustrator
Concept

Its Buisness started as a way of processing the strangeness of professional life — the unspoken rules, the odd rituals, the gap between what work looks like and what it feels like. Illustration became the medium because it could hold absurdity without deflating it.

Outcome

The series uses a flat, composed visual style that treats its subjects with complete seriousness — which is where most of the humour lives. Each piece is a small portrait of a very specific professional feeling.
