Upcyclo

2025 Connection Digital Print

A digital and print system for a circular fashion platform, encouraging conscious consumption and creative reuse through community-driven exchange.

Role Designer
Tools Figma, Illustrator
Concept

Upcyclo was built around the idea that clothing already has a life before you own it — and another after you're done. The platform needed to make that story visible, turning exchange into something that felt less like a transaction and more like passing something forward.

Outcome

The visual language pulled from archival fashion photography and handmade zine aesthetics — printed lookbooks, tag systems, and a digital interface that felt curated rather than algorithmic.
