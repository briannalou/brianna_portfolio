With Habit

2026 Product Print

A wellness and habit tracking product combining print and digital touchpoints to help users build sustainable routines through gentle daily structure.

Role Product Designer
Tools Figma, Illustrator
Concept

The project began with a simple question: how do small, repeated actions compound into meaningful change? With Habit explores the tactile ritual of daily tracking — making the invisible visible through print and digital touchpoints designed to fit quietly into everyday life.

Process

Prototyping moved between analog and digital — hand-drawn tracker templates, A5 booklets, and a companion app that translated logged habits into gentle weekly reflections. The goal was always texture: something worth picking up.
