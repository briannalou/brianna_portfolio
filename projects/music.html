Music+

A music discovery and streaming app reimagining how listeners explore, collect, and connect with sound across taste profiles and shared listening sessions.

2024 App Figma Ae
Concept

Music+ was a response to the way streaming flattened music into background noise. The goal was to restore some of the ceremony of listening — the ritual of choosing, the weight of a record, the way a shared playlist becomes a kind of language between people.

Process

Early explorations focused on the physicality of collection — crate-digging translated to digital stacks, liner notes as a design element, and social listening that didn't require a feed.
