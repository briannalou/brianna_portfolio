Inside Out

2023 Website

A nature-inspired digital series examining the boundary between interior and exterior spaces, exploring what it means to feel at home in an environment.

Tools Figma, Photoshop
Concept

Inside Out began with the observation that the spaces we inhabit quietly shape who we become in them. The series explores thresholds — windows, doorways, edges — as places where inside and outside blur and identity becomes negotiable.

Reflection

Working with natural textures and organic forms shifted the project away from clean digital rendering toward something more tactile. The final pieces were meant to feel like something found rather than made.
