Mirrored Universe

A multimedia project blending film, print, and digital to explore themes of reflection and mirrored realities — how we perceive ourselves through the lens of another world.

2023 Motion Premiere Pro Photoshop
Concept

Mirrored Universe started as a thought experiment: if you could step into the reflection, what would you find? The project used film, print, and digital layering to construct an alternate version of familiar spaces — recognizable but slightly wrong.

Reflection

The decision to work across media — shooting on film, scanning prints, manipulating digitally, and printing again — introduced generation loss as an intentional design element. Each layer of translation distorted the original a little more.
