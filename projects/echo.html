Echo

2025 Connection Digital App

A connection app designed to bring people together through shared moments, ambient presence, and gentle nudges that reduce the friction of staying in touch.

Role UX/UI Designer
Tools Figma, Protopie
Concept

Echo started as a response to the friction of keeping up — the mental overhead of remembering to reach out, of wondering whether someone is thinking of you too. The app was designed to feel less like a communication tool and more like a quiet shared room.

Reflection

The hardest design challenge was restraint — building features that felt present without demanding attention. Ambient presence cues, passive check-ins, and optional nudges replaced feeds and notifications entirely.
